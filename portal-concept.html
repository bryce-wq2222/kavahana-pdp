Questions

How do I change what is in my box?

Open Subscription in the menu, then Edit. You can swap size, add a bag or change the flavour any time before your next charge.

Can I skip a month?

Yes. Hit Skip on any scheduled date above. Skipping keeps your place in the Fam, so your rewards carry on.

What happens to my rewards if I pause?

Nothing. A pause holds your place. Cancelling resets you to month 1 if you come back.
