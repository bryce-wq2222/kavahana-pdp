Kava 101

Most people have never
had real kava

If you have only seen it in a little bottle at a gas station, you have not had this. Here is the whole thing in about ninety seconds.

What it is

A root drink, not a supplement

Kava comes from the root of a plant grown in the South Pacific and Hawaii, where it has been enjoyed for over 3,000 years as a family drink. It is the national drink of Fiji. People drink it the way other cultures drink tea, together, in the evening, to talk.

It is not alcohol and it is not a mocktail. It is its own thing, with its own history, and it does something you can feel.

The tingle

Your mouth will tingle. That is the point.

The kava plant is a member of the peppercorn family, so a few seconds after your first sip your lips and tongue go tingly. It surprises everybody the first time.

It is the signature of real kava, and the first thing regulars look for.

The timing

10 to 20 minutes, then 1 to 2 hours

Many people feel relaxed, smiley and social about 10 to 20 minutes in, and it tends to last 1 to 2 hours. Some of our bar regulars call it smile juice.

Then it is over, and you go to bed, and you wake up clear. No crash and no hangover to pay for it.

How it is made

Cold pressed from fresh root

Kava Nectar is made by cold pressing fresh kava root and drying that into a powder, so it is the nectar of the root. 15 seconds with a whisk and it is ready, with no pulp and no fibers.

A convenient way to enjoy a drink that has been prepared by hand in the islands for thousands of years.

5 to 7 years in the ground

The plants grow 5 to 7 years in volcanic soil before harvest, and the whole plant is lifted for its roots. That patience is the product, and it is the reason kava of this grade costs what it does.

Questions

Before you start

I tried kava before and felt nothing. Why?

Almost certainly reverse tolerance. It is not unusual for people to enjoy 2 to 3 servings, especially in the beginning. It is the most common reason somebody writes off kava after a single serving.

What is it like to drink?

Earthy, and it makes your mouth tingle. Nothing is added to it. Most people mix it with cold coconut water, or pineapple and coconut milk, both of which are in the recipe guide that comes in your kit.

Is there kratom in it?

No. Kava Nectar is made from kava root and nothing else. Kava and kratom are two different plants. Kava is in the peppercorn family, which is where the tingle comes from. If you have only tried the small bottles sold in gas stations and smoke shops, those are often kava mixed with something else, and that is a different experience from this one.

What about the liver?

That is actually a misconception. Natural kava root has been enjoyed in the South Pacific and Hawaii for centuries as a family drink, and it is Fiji's national drink too. The concern people have read about traces back to the early 2000s, when a pharmaceutical company processed kava into an extract, which led to bans in Europe. Those bans have since been lifted, including in Germany, where a court overturned it.

Can I drive after drinking it?

Kava doesn't intoxicate you like alcohol does, but just like with a strong tea you could feel sleepy, so enjoy it when you don't have to drive.

Is there an age restriction?

There is no legal age restriction in the US, and kava has been enjoyed as a family drink for over 3,000 years. We still recommend 18 and over for a first time, because it has an effect you can feel.

Can I drink it while pregnant?

Not recommended, due to lack of studies.

Is it habit forming?

Natural kava root is non-addictive and non-intoxicating. It also works on reverse tolerance, which is the opposite of how tolerance normally builds.
